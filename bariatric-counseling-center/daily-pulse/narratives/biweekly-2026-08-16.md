# Biweekly briefing -- 2026-08-16

### The window in one sentence

Over the 14 days ending August 14, sessions grew 17.6% from the first week to the second, driven by a clear acceleration in the back half of the window -- with August 12 posting the strongest single day (37 sessions, double the 28-day median). That acceleration coincides with the August 13 batch update across the eating disorder and food relationship cluster, and the channel mix is running unusually heavy on Organic Search relative to BCC's baseline, which is the more important signal here.

---

### Volume + composition

The window produced 296 sessions, 262 users, and 990 pageviews across 14 days, for a per-day average of 21.1 sessions and 18.7 users. Pageviews-per-session runs roughly 3.3, which suggests meaningful depth of engagement -- people are not landing and leaving. GSC impressions and clicks both read zero for the entire window, which is a data gap rather than a ranking collapse; this is almost certainly a GSC API connection issue and should be treated as missing data, not as evidence that organic visibility has disappeared.

Channel composition is the headline story in the volume data. Organic Search delivered 143 of 296 sessions -- that is 48% of total traffic, nearly double BCC's expected 25% baseline. Direct traffic came in at 110 sessions (37%), well below the expected 60%. Referral added 33 sessions (11%), roughly in line with baseline. Organic Social, Email, and Unassigned together account for 13 sessions and are not meaningful movers this window. The Organic Search dominance is notable and encouraging -- it suggests content is pulling in people who were not already familiar with BCC. The corresponding drop in Direct share is not a loss of that audience; it likely reflects that the denominator grew and Organic filled the new space. The low Direct share is worth watching over the next 14 days to confirm that pattern holds rather than flipping back.

---

### Trend (first half vs second half)

Sessions in the first half (August 1-7) totaled 136. Sessions in the second half (August 8-14) totaled 160 -- a 17.6% lift. That is a clean accelerating trend. Pageviews ran slightly the other direction (505 first half vs 485 second half), but the gap is narrow enough to be noise, and it does not undercut the session growth story. If anything, the combination of more sessions and roughly equal pageviews means the per-session depth is leveling out, which is normal as new-to-site visitors from Organic Search make up more of the mix -- they often browse fewer pages on a first visit.

The acceleration was not smooth. August 8-9 dipped sharply (11-15 sessions both days, with session duration spiking on the 9th, suggesting a very small number of highly engaged users rather than broad traffic). August 10 and 12 both hit double-the-median levels and drove the second-half average up significantly. The closing three days of the window (August 12-14) all ran above the 28-day median consistently, which is the most meaningful part of that trend -- it is not a single spike, it is a new floor forming.

The most significant activity in this window was the August 13 batch update, which touched 20 pages and posts across two distinct content clusters. The eating disorder and food relationship cluster received updates on at least nine pages: `/stop-the-cycle-of-binge-eating-you-dont-have-to-face-this-alone/`, `/binge-eating-therapy-in-san-antonio-tx/`, `/food-addiction-treatment-in-san-antonio-tx/`, `/compulsive-overeating-counseling-san-antonio-tx/`, `/binge-eating-disorder-treatment-san-antonio/`, `/night-eating-syndrome-treatment-in-san-antonio-tx/`, `/food-addiction-treatment-in-texas/`, and the pre-bariatric binge eating screening post. The `/testimonials/` page, the hydration post, and the emotional eating coping skills post were also updated the same day.

GA4 session attribution is near zero across the board, with `/post-bariatric-surgery-support/` and `/professional-weight-loss-therapy/` each showing 1 attributed session -- the only pages with any signal at all. This is expected given the recency; none of these pages have had enough time to be re-crawled, re-indexed, and served at meaningful volume. The weight loss therapy variants -- `/professional-weight-loss-therapy-2/` and `/professional-weight-loss-therapy-fb/` -- appear to be landing page variants rather than organic content plays. These likely serve paid or social traffic and should not be evaluated against organic ranking metrics; the evaluation question for those pages is conversion rate and downstream inquiry volume, which sits outside this briefing's data scope. Backlink totals show 4 referring domains, 12 inbound links, and 5 edu links -- the edu link count is disproportionately strong for a site this size and represents real authority. No new backlinks are attributed to this window specifically.

### What this means + what to do next

1. **Establish a GSC data quality check before next rollup.** Zero impressions across 14 consecutive days is a strong signal that the GSC API connection is broken or the property is misconfigured, not that the site has lost all search visibility. Resolving this is the single most important infrastructure task for the coming week -- without GSC data, efficacy analysis for the August 13 cluster will be blind when it matters most (days 10-21 post-update).

2. **Build internal links into the August 13 eating disorder cluster now, before rankings move.** These pages were updated as a batch, which means they share topical relevance but may not yet link to each other meaningfully. Adding cross-links between `/binge-eating-disorder-treatment-san-antonio/`, `/binge-eating-therapy-in-san-antonio-tx/`, `/food-addiction-treatment-in-san-antonio-tx/`, and the pre-bariatric binge eating screening post creates a crawlable cluster that signals topical depth to Google -- and that work is worth doing this week, before the pages start accumulating ranking positions that would make changes riskier.

3. **Investigate the Organic Search channel share carefully.** Running at 48% vs a 25% baseline is a meaningful departure. Pull the specific queries and landing pages driving that share in GA4 for the window -- if this is the eating disorder or bariatric cluster performing, that is confirmation the content strategy is working and worth accelerating. If it is unexplained or concentrated on a single branded query, that changes the interpretation.

4. **Audit the weight loss therapy landing page variants for consolidation opportunity.** Four versions of what appears to be a therapy landing page (`/professional-weight-loss-therapy/`, `-2`, `-fb`, and `/expert-weight-loss-therapy/`) all updated the same day suggests an active testing or audience-segmentation effort. If these are not actively serving differentiated paid traffic, duplicate content across four near-identical slugs is a crawl budget and canonicalization issue worth resolving -- especially as Organic Search share grows.

5. **Flag August 12's session-duration drop for follow-up.** August 12 was the window's peak session day (37 sessions, double the median) but average session duration collapsed 58% below median to 52 seconds. High volume with low engagement on the same day suggests a traffic source that drove clicks but not real intent -- possibly an untagged social share or a referral from a low-context source. Identifying that source will help determine whether the August 12 spike was meaningful audience growth or a one-day artifact.
