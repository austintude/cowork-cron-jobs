The window is clearly declining: 111 sessions in the first half (Aug 18--20, 3 days) versus 83 in the second half (Aug 21--24, 4 days), a -25.2% drop. Translated to per-day rates, that's 37 sessions/day in the first half versus 20.8 sessions/day in the second half -- a nearly 2x difference. The Aug 19--20 spike (both days flagged CRITICAL across sessions, users, new users, and pageviews, running 75--122% above their 28-day medians) appears to have been a discrete event -- likely a social post, an email send, or a referral that drove a short burst of new users who didn't return. The session-duration pattern reinforces this: duration was elevated on Aug 18, then dropped sharply on Aug 19--20 as the volume spike arrived (more people, browsing more shallowly), and stayed low through the week. The weekend trough on Aug 22--23 is typical for a service business, but the depth of the drop -- sessions down 38--51% below the 28-day median -- was more pronounced than usual. Aug 24 partially recovered with pageviews running 36% above median, which is a mildly positive note to close on.
