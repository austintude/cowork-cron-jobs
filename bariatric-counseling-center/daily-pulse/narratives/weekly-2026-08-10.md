# Weekly briefing -- 2026-08-10

### The window in one sentence

The week of August 2--8 showed a meaningful acceleration from first half to second half, with sessions up 43% and pageviews up 65% across the two periods. The spike in engagement on August 4--5 -- including a 240% surge in average session duration on the 5th -- suggests the August 6 wave of content updates landed on an audience that was already warming up, though GSC data being entirely absent this window makes it impossible to confirm whether organic search is driving any of it.

---

### Volume + composition

Over the 7-day window (August 2--8), the site recorded 141 sessions, 123 users, and 526 pageviews. Per-day averages came in at 20.1 sessions and 17.6 users -- both roughly in line with the 28-day median, though the distribution was far from even. The window opened weak (12 sessions, 33 pageviews on the 2nd) and built to its peak on the 5th (162 pageviews, average session duration of 408 seconds -- nearly 7 minutes). GSC reported zero impressions and zero clicks across all seven days, which is almost certainly a data lag or connection issue rather than a real signal. Do not read organic search as dead; verify the GSC feed before drawing any conclusions.

Channel composition this week flipped BCC's baseline on its head. Organic Search was the top channel at 72 sessions (51% of total), while Direct came in second at 47 sessions (33%). That's a notable reversal from the expected 60/25 split. Referral added 17 sessions (12%), Organic Social brought 4, and Email and Unassigned each contributed 1. The Organic Search dominance is worth paying attention to -- without GSC data to back it up, it's hard to know whether this reflects genuine ranking gains or some tagging anomaly. The low Direct share relative to baseline could mean an email or internal campaign didn't go out this week, or it could mean Direct traffic is simply returning to a more accurate baseline after a period of inflated untagged traffic.

---

### Trend (first half vs second half)

The window is accelerating, and the numbers are clear about it. The first half (August 2--4) produced 58 sessions and 198 pageviews. The second half (August 5--8) produced 83 sessions and 328 pageviews -- a 43% session increase and a 65% pageview increase over four days instead of three. That's not just more days; the per-day pace genuinely picked up.

What's striking is the engagement quality. August 4--5 showed session duration well above the 28-day median -- 46% above on the 4th, 240% above on the 5th. People who came to the site mid-week were spending real time there. August 6--8 pulled back on duration but remained solid on session and user volume (27 sessions on both the 4th and 7th hit the top of the range for the window). The pattern suggests the bulk August 6 content updates -- which touched the homepage, About page, the weight loss medications page, and several posts -- may have been preceded by organic activity that was already building. That's a healthy sequence if it holds.

### Launches + efficacy

August 6 was by far the most active deployment day in this window, with 16 items modified -- pages and posts spanning core service pages (homepage, About, the bariatric surgery counseling page, the psychological evaluation page, Coordinate with Medical Providers) and content posts (Emotional Eating and Coping Skills, Goal-Setting for Weight Loss, How to Identify Self-Sabotage Causes, Movement: Where to Start, and others). Four days is not enough time to judge whether these updates moved rankings, but two items from that batch are worth watching closely right now.

The weight loss medications page (/understanding-weight-loss-medications-wegovy-ozempic-zepbound-and-mounjaro/) is the standout in the entire top-launches list. It has accumulated 31,992 GSC impressions and 94 clicks -- by a wide margin the highest-traffic page in the efficacy data. At a 0.3% CTR it is being seen constantly but not clicked, which suggests the page is ranking for high-volume informational queries where BCC's title or meta description isn't compelling searchers to choose this result. That's a CTR optimization problem, not a content problem. The Coordinate with Medical Providers page (/ourprogram/coordinate-with-medical-providers/) shows 2,800 impressions and only 11 clicks (0.4% CTR) with 3 sessions -- lower volume but the same CTR gap pattern.

The 30-day efficacy rollup shows 212,104 GSC impressions and 1,016 clicks attributed across all launches, with 840 GA4 sessions attributed -- but zero pages ranking in the top 10 in GSC. That zero deserves a direct callout: the site is generating substantial impression volume, which means pages are being seen in search results, but none are cracking the first page. The backlink picture is modest but not nothing -- 4 referring domains, 12 inbound links, and 5 of those are .edu links, which is the highest-quality link category available. No new backlinks appear to have been earned specifically within this 7-day window based on the available ledger data.

### What this means + what to do next

1. **Investigate and fix the GSC data gap before anything else.** Zero impressions across 7 days is almost certainly a feed or credentials issue, not a real-world signal. Until this is resolved, you cannot evaluate organic search performance with confidence. This is the highest-priority diagnostic item for the coming week.

2. **Run a title tag and meta description audit on the weight loss medications page.** With nearly 32,000 impressions and a 0.3% CTR, this page is ranking for queries people are actually searching -- but something in the snippet is not earning the click. Test a more direct, benefit-forward title and a meta description that speaks specifically to people seeking guidance on GLP-1 medications in relation to bariatric care. Even moving CTR from 0.3% to 1% would triple clicks from that page alone.

3. **Investigate what drove the August 4--5 engagement spike before attributing it to content.** Average session duration of 408 seconds on August 5 is a real signal that someone (or a group of people) was reading deeply. Check GA4 for which pages drove that engagement -- if it was a specific post or service page, that's a content pattern worth repeating. If it was concentrated among a handful of sessions, it may not generalize.

4. **Build an internal linking pass into the August 6 content batch.** The homepage, About, and the two service pages (bariatric counseling and psychological evaluation) were all updated the same day. If those updates didn't include fresh internal links pointing toward the weight loss medications page and the Coordinate with Medical Providers page, add them now. Those two pages have the highest impression volume and need session support from pages people are already landing on.

5. **Develop a second outreach to any .edu referring domain that has already linked to BCC.** Five .edu links is a meaningful foundation -- those institutions have already decided BCC is a credible reference. A follow-up pitch (a new resource, an updated page, a request to link a more specific page) has a much higher success rate than cold outreach and would build on the highest-authority link equity the site currently holds.
