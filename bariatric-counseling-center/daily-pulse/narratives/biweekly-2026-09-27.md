# Biweekly briefing -- 2026-09-27

### The window in one sentence

Over the 14 days of September 12--25, sessions held perfectly flat (219 in each half) while the final days of the window showed sharp pageview spikes and session duration anomalies that suggest the Sep 23--25 content push is starting to pull engagement. GSC data is unavailable at the site aggregate level for this window, but page-level data on key modified posts confirms meaningful impression volume is accumulating.

---

### Volume + composition

The site logged 438 sessions, 391 users, and 1,427 pageviews across 14 days -- averaging 31.3 sessions and 27.9 users per day. Those numbers are steady rather than impressive, sitting close to the 28-day median that the anomaly engine keeps referencing (~30 sessions/day). Pageviews tell a slightly more interesting story: the window ended strong, with Sep 22 (150 pageviews), Sep 23 (101), and Sep 25 (181) pulling the second-half average well above the mid-window trough.

Channel composition is noticeably off from BCC's baseline. Direct came in at 193 sessions (44% of total) and Organic Search at 176 (40%), which is a meaningful shift -- Organic is punching closer to Direct than the expected 60/25 split. That inversion suggests the content modification work over the past several months is translating into real search-driven visits. Email accounted for 37 sessions (8%), Referral for 22 (5%), and 19 sessions (4%) are sitting as Unassigned -- that Unassigned bucket is worth watching, since it often signals social or email traffic arriving without proper UTM tagging.

The high Organic Search share relative to baseline is the channel story of this window. It is not a dramatic spike; it looks more like a sustained drift upward that has quietly closed the gap with Direct. If this holds into the next 14 days, it represents a structural shift worth naming explicitly.

### Trend (first half vs second half)

Sessions split exactly 219 / 219 between the two halves -- a flat trend by the numbers. But flat sessions with a second-half pageview increase (707 vs 720) hints at something subtler: the people arriving in the second half are viewing more pages per visit, not just the same amount. Average session duration anomalies reinforce this -- Sep 23 came in at 136% above median, Sep 24 at 381% above, and Sep 25 at 88% above. That pattern almost certainly reflects the burst of page modifications on Sep 23--25 pulling returning users into longer browsing sessions.

The honest read is this: volume is flat and that is not alarming for a site at this traffic level, but the quality signals in the second half (duration, pageviews-per-session) are moving in the right direction. The mid-window trough -- Sep 19 (25 sessions, 62 pageviews) and Sep 20 (24 sessions, 45 pageviews) -- is concerning on its own, but those two days correspond to no launches and no apparent promotional activity, which likely explains the dip. The Sep 22 and Sep 25 rebounds suggest the site responds meaningfully to content activity.

### Launches + efficacy

This window was heavy on modifications rather than new page creation, and the last four days (Sep 22--25) account for nearly all of it. On Sep 23, six pages were updated including core service pages -- /emotional-eating-therapy-in-san-antonio-tx/, /overeating-treatment-in-san-antonio-tx/, Dr. Sara Hamilton's bio page, Testimonials, and two program sub-pages (Meet with Registered Dietitians, Learn to Cook Differently). On Sep 24, two posts were modified: the hydration article at /healthy-living-2/why-hydration-is-essential-for-weight-loss-and-management/ and the gastroparesis post at /diet-and-nutrition/gut-health/gastroparesis-aka-stomach-paralysis/. On Sep 25, four more pages were modified including /what-is-a-weight-loss-counselor/, /nutritional-counseling-in-san-antonio-tx/, /bariatric-therapy-in-san-antonio-tx/, and /start/.

The hydration post is the standout performer in this window. At just three days post-modification, it has accumulated 344,397 GSC impressions and 1,716 clicks (0.5% CTR), driving 369 GA4 sessions -- by far the highest attributed session count in the top launches list. That CTR is low relative to impression volume, which suggests the page is ranking broadly but the title or snippet is not compelling enough searchers to click at the rate it could. That is worth addressing. The gastroparesis post is also pulling real numbers -- 20,351 impressions, 49 clicks, 20 sessions -- but a 0.2% CTR on that impression volume points to the same snippet problem.

The /what-is-a-weight-loss-counselor/ page shows 1,082 impressions and 7 sessions in just two days, with a 1.3% CTR that is meaningfully better than the posts above. The /nutritional-counseling-in-san-antonio-tx/ page has a striking 3,854 impressions with zero clicks -- that is a title and meta description problem that should be resolved quickly. Backlinks show 12 total inbound links across 4 referring domains, including 5 .edu links, which is a real asset for a site at this domain age. No new backlinks were added in the window based on available data.

### What this means + what to do next

1. **Rewrite the meta descriptions and title tags for the hydration post and the gastroparesis post.** Combined, those two pages are generating over 360,000 impressions with CTRs under 0.5%. Even moving to 1% CTR on the hydration post alone would roughly triple its click volume. This is the highest-leverage SEO action available right now and should be prioritized before any new page production.

2. **Fix the /nutritional-counseling-in-san-antonio-tx/ snippet before the week is out.** 3,854 impressions with zero clicks at two days post-launch means something in the title or description is actively failing to connect with searchers. Pull the GSC query data for that page, identify the top impression-driving queries, and rewrite the title and meta to match searcher language.

3. **Investigate the Unassigned channel and audit UTM coverage on social and email sends.** 19 sessions (4%) sitting in Unassigned over 14 days suggests either organic social or email sends are going out without proper tagging. At BCC's traffic scale, recovering that attribution matters -- if those sessions are actually Email or Organic Social, the channel composition picture changes and informs where to keep investing.

4. **Develop an internal linking plan that connects the Sep 23--25 modified service pages to each other and to the high-impression blog posts.** The emotional eating, overeating, and bariatric therapy pages were all modified in the same window. If they are not pointing to each other and to the hydration and gastroparesis content, you are leaving topical authority on the table. A simple internal linking audit across those eight pages would take one focused session.

5. **Leverage the .edu backlink profile.** Five .edu links is an above-average ratio for a regional therapy practice and signals that academic or university health sources have already cited BCC. Identify which .edu domains those are and consider whether any have related resource pages or faculty health content where a second or third link could be pitched. That model -- a trusted institution already linking, a warm door to open -- is more efficient than cold outreach.
