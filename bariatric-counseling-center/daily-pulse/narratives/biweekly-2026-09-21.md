# Biweekly briefing -- 2026-09-21

### The window in one sentence

Over the 14-day window (Sep 6--19), the site logged 465 sessions at a daily average of 33.2 -- a serviceable baseline but one that declined 25% from the first week to the second, suggesting the early-window traffic spikes were event-driven rather than structural growth. The biggest content push of the period -- 19 page modifications touching the entire eating-disorder cluster -- shipped on September 18, putting measurable impact squarely outside this window.

---

### Volume + composition

Total volume for the window: 465 sessions, 428 users, 1,332 pageviews, and zero GSC impressions or clicks. That last figure is notable and almost certainly a data pipeline issue rather than a real zero -- the 30-day efficacy rollup shows 1,019,175 attributed GSC impressions and 5,241 clicks, so GSC data simply did not populate for this window. Do not read the zero-impression figure as an organic collapse; treat it as a reporting gap that needs to be resolved before next week's briefing.

Channel breakdown: Direct led at 212 sessions (45.6%), Organic Search came in at 164 (35.3%), Email at 67 (14.4%), Referral at 13 (2.8%), and Organic Social plus Unassigned split the remainder. Compared to BCC's baseline of roughly 60% Direct / 25% Organic Search / 10% Referral, this window shows Direct running notably below baseline and Organic Search running notably above it. Email at 14.4% is also elevated -- roughly 4--5 points above a typical baseline -- which lines up with the Sep 8 and Sep 11 session spikes and suggests one or more email sends during the first week drove meaningful traffic. The Referral figure (2.8%) is below baseline, which may simply be a quiet fortnight for referral sources rather than a structural change.

---

### Trend (first half vs second half)

The window is declining. The first half (Sep 6--12) produced 266 sessions; the second half (Sep 13--19) produced 199 -- a 25.2% drop. Pageviews, interestingly, were exactly equal: 666 in each half. That pageviews-per-session ratio rising in the second half (roughly 3.35 vs. 2.50) is the one genuinely positive signal here -- people who did come in the second half engaged more deeply, which is consistent with the anomaly data showing average session duration running 89--260% above the 28-day median on multiple second-half days (Sep 14, 15, 16, 18, 19).

The honest read: volume is declining, engagement quality is improving. That combination often points to a shift from broad-reach traffic (email blast, social share) fading out while a smaller audience of more intentional visitors -- likely people arriving via Organic Search on specific service queries -- remains. The Sep 10 crash to 7 sessions (76% below median) followed immediately by the Sep 11 spike to 64 (117% above median) is almost certainly one email send landing a day later than expected, not a meaningful trend signal on its own.

### Launches + efficacy

The headline shipping event of this window was September 18: 19 pages modified in a single day, touching essentially every major node in the eating-disorder and related-services cluster -- `/eating-disorder-treatment-in-san-antonio-tx/`, `/eating-disorder-therapist-in-san-antonio-tx/`, `/eating-disorder-counseling-in-san-antonio-tx/`, `/therapy-for-eating-disorders-in-san-antonio-tx/`, `/eating-disorder-iop-in-san-antonio-tx/`, `/eating-disorder-group-therapy-san-antonio-tx/`, `/telehealth-eating-disorder-treatment-in-texas/`, `/eating-disorder-therapist-near-me/`, `/eating-disorder-referrals-for-providers-in-san-antonio-tx/`, `/adhd-and-emotional-eating-in-san-antonio-tx/`, `/overeaters-anonymous-support-san-antonio-tx/`, plus the homepage (`/`), the `/start/` intake page, and several supporting pages. At 3 days post-launch, impact data is essentially unavailable -- this is normal. Google typically takes 1--3 weeks to re-crawl and re-rank modified pages at this scale.

The one new page published in the window -- `/video-lightbox-test/` -- appears to be a development or UI test rather than a content asset. It has 2 GA4 sessions and no GSC impressions. That slug will likely need to be either noindexed or removed from the public URL space if it was not intended to rank.

The `/adhd-and-emotional-eating-in-san-antonio-tx/` page is the only modified page showing any GSC signal at 3 days post-update: 211 impressions and 6 clicks at a 2.8% CTR. That CTR is actually solid for a page this fresh after a modification -- it suggests the page was already indexed at a position where people would click, and the update did not disrupt that. Worth watching closely next week. The homepage (`/`) carries the heaviest cumulative load in the entire site -- 338,659 GSC impressions and 1,044 GA4 sessions in its attributed window -- so the Sep 18 homepage modification is the highest-stakes single change in this window. Any positive or negative ranking movement on branded and near-branded queries in the next 2 weeks traces directly back to that edit.

On backlinks: the site currently has 4 referring domains, 12 inbound links, and 5 .edu links with zero competitor links. No new backlinks were recorded as earned within this specific window.

### What this means + what to do next

1. **Monitor the Sep 18 cluster update for ranking movement, not just traffic.** The 19-page modification touched pages that collectively represent BCC's core organic search footprint. Pull GSC position data for each modified URL at the 7-day and 14-day marks and compare to pre-edit baselines. If any pages dropped position, isolate which edits correlated -- the scale of the update means it is worth verifying nothing was inadvertently changed in title tags, canonical tags, or internal link structure.

2. **Investigate and resolve the GSC data gap.** All 14 days of this window show zero GSC impressions, which contradicts the 30-day rollup. This likely points to a reporting or API connection issue rather than a real traffic event. If it is not resolved, the next trend briefing will be flying blind on organic search momentum at exactly the moment the Sep 18 updates start producing ranking signals.

3. **Audit the `/video-lightbox-test/` page.** If this was a development test, it should be noindexed or password-protected. A publicly indexable URL with the word "test" in the slug signals amateur site hygiene to Google and could attract crawl budget unnecessarily.

4. **Identify the email send(s) behind the Sep 8 and Sep 11 spikes and evaluate for replication.** Email drove roughly 14% of all sessions this window -- meaningfully above baseline -- and the spike pattern strongly suggests a deliberate send. If that send targeted existing contacts or a referral partner list, it is worth assessing whether a cadence of 1--2 sends per month could sustain a higher Direct/Email floor going into Q4.

5. **Plan a .edu backlink outreach push.** The site has 5 .edu links already, which is a real asset at this domain's size. That is the model that is working. Identify 2--3 UTSA, UTHSCSA, or community college health department contacts where BCC's eating-disorder or behavioral health content could serve as a legitimate resource citation, and draft an outreach sequence for the coming two weeks.
