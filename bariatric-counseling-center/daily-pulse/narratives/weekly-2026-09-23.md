That quality signal is unusual and worth tracing to a specific traffic source or page. Pull a segment in GA4 for sessions with duration over 180 seconds from September 15-18 and see which pages they landed on and which channel sent them. If a specific page is pulling deep-reading sessions, it likely needs stronger internal links and a clear next step for visitors.
