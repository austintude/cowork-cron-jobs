# Biweekly briefing -- 2026-07-26

### The window in one sentence

Over the 14 days ending July 24, sessions ran slightly below baseline at 17.4 per day -- but a major content push on July 23 (19 pages modified) produced a dramatic pageview spike in the final two days that signals something meaningful may be starting to take hold. GSC data is showing zero impressions across the entire window, which is the most important unresolved question right now.

---

### Volume + composition

The site generated 244 sessions, 219 users, and 2,158 pageviews over the 14-day window, with a per-day average of 17.4 sessions and 15.6 users. Those session numbers are roughly in line with BCC's baseline, neither a standout nor a concern on their own. What is unusual is the pageview-to-session ratio: 2,158 pageviews across only 244 sessions works out to about 8.8 pages per session, which is far above typical. That number is almost entirely explained by the July 23-24 spike -- those two days alone produced 1,544 of the window's pageviews, with average session durations of 447 seconds (July 23) and 250 seconds (July 24). Someone -- or a small group of people -- spent serious time on the site on those days.

Channel composition shows Direct at 43% (105 sessions), Organic Search at 28% (68), and Referral at 20% (49). Direct is below BCC's expected ~60% baseline, while Referral is running high at double its typical share. Organic Search is slightly above its ~25% baseline. The elevated Referral number is worth tracking -- it may reflect activity from the 12 inbound links currently in the backlink profile, including 5 .edu links, which is a real asset if any of those are driving consistent traffic. The 4 AI Assistant sessions are a small but notable signal worth watching over coming windows as that channel grows across the industry.

GSC impressions and clicks show zero across all 14 days. This is almost certainly a data pipeline issue or a GSC property configuration problem rather than a sign that the site has fallen out of Google's index -- the 30-day efficacy rollup attributes 351,072 impressions and 1,475 clicks to tracked pages, which confirms indexing is active. This needs to be resolved before the next rollup window so the trend data is usable.

---

### Trend (first half vs second half)

On sessions alone, the window is declining: the first half (July 11-17) produced 141 sessions versus 103 in the second half (July 18-24), a drop of 27%. That is a concerning number if taken at face value. However, the session drop in the second half is heavily distorted by two very low days -- July 18 (8 sessions, -59% vs. median) and July 19 (2 sessions, -90% vs. median) -- that appear to be anomalies rather than trend signals. The cause of those two-day troughs is worth investigating; they correlate with a Friday-Saturday pattern, which suggests normal weekend drop-off amplified by something else.

Pageviews tell a completely different story. The first half produced 428 pageviews; the second half produced 1,730 -- a 304% increase. That delta is almost entirely attributable to the July 23-24 surge that followed the content push. The most honest read of this window is: sessions are flat-to-soft, but depth of engagement jumped sharply at the close of the window. Whether that pageview spike represents crawlers, internal review, or actual users arriving from the refreshed pages will become clearer in the next 7-10 days as GSC data catches up.

---

### Launches + efficacy

July 23 was a significant production day. Nineteen pages were modified in a single push, covering a wide range of BCC's service areas: the homepage, eating disorder treatment and IOP, binge eating therapy and treatment, emotional eating therapy, compulsive overeating counseling, food addiction treatment, nutritional counseling, bariatric surgery counseling, body confidence counseling, holistic weight loss counseling, the Foundayo behavioral support page, and an overeaters anonymous support page. That is a substantial content investment in a single day, and the immediate pageview response -- 592 views on July 23, 952 on July 24 -- suggests the site was being actively reviewed post-publish, whether by internal staff, crawlers, or early organic visitors.

Of the pages with measurable early GSC data, the homepage (/\) is the clear standout: 47,094 impressions and 470 GA4 sessions with a 0.7% CTR. That CTR is low relative to impression volume, which suggests the homepage meta title and description may not be pulling clicks efficiently even when Google is surfacing the page. The /nutritional-counseling-in-san-antonio-tx/ page is showing 3,854 impressions with zero clicks -- that is a significant gap and a clear CTR or snippet problem that warrants attention now, not later. The /holistic-weight-loss-counseling-in-san-antonio-tx/ page has 318 impressions and zero clicks -- same pattern, smaller scale. The remaining pages modified on July 23 show either zero impressions or no data yet, which is expected at three days post-publish.

The 30-day efficacy rollup shows 351,072 total GSC impressions and 1,475 clicks attributed across all tracked launches, which works out to a 0.42% average CTR. That is low across the board and reinforces the idea that impression volume is not the limiting factor -- converting impressions to clicks is. No backlinks were earned within the 14-day window itself, though the existing profile of 12 inbound links across 4 referring domains, including 5 .edu links, remains a meaningful foundation.

---

### What this means + what to do next

1. **Audit the GSC property configuration this week.** Fourteen consecutive days of zero GSC impressions in the daily data feed, against a backdrop of 351,072 impressions in the 30-day rollup, suggests a data connection problem between the GSC property and this reporting pipeline. Resolve this before the next rollup window -- without working GSC data, it is not possible to evaluate whether the July 23 content push is gaining organic traction.

2. **Prioritize title and meta description rewrites for /nutritional-counseling-in-san-antonio-tx/ and /holistic-weight-loss-counseling-in-san-antonio-tx/.** Combined, these pages are generating nearly 4,200 impressions with zero clicks. That is not a ranking problem -- Google is finding and showing these pages. The problem is the snippet is not compelling enough to earn the click. Test revised titles that lead with the person's situation rather than the service name.

3. **Build internal linking across the July 23 cluster.** Nineteen pages were updated in one push, but if they are not linked to each other and to the homepage in a structured way, Google has less signal about how they relate. Spend time this week mapping which pages in the eating disorder, binge eating, and overeating cluster point to each other, and fill the gaps. This is likely the highest-leverage technical SEO action available right now.

4. **Investigate the July 18-19 traffic trough.** Two consecutive days of near-zero sessions (-59% and -90% vs. median) are unusual even for a weekend. Check whether a plugin update, a caching configuration change, or a tracking script issue coincided with those dates. If it was a tracking gap rather than real traffic loss, that matters for how the half-over-half trend numbers are interpreted.

5. **Develop a plan for the .edu backlink asset.** Five .edu links across 4 referring domains is a genuine differentiator for a local counseling practice. Identify which institution(s) those links come from, confirm the links are still active and contextually placed, and consider whether a relationship exists there that could yield a second link, a co-authored resource, or a referral pathway. This is a strategic exploration for the coming week, not a same-day task.
