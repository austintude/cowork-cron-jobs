# Weekly briefing -- 2026-08-13

### The window in one sentence

Over the 7-day window (Aug 5--11), BCC logged 140 sessions at a steady 20/day average, with Organic Search running well above its typical share and a major batch of page modifications on Aug 10 producing the week's single biggest traffic day. GSC data is missing for the full window, which limits how cleanly we can attribute what's working.

---

### Volume + composition

The window totaled 140 sessions, 123 users, and 530 pageviews across seven days, averaging 20 sessions and 17.6 users per day. Those numbers are modest in absolute terms but consistent with BCC's typical volume. Pageviews per session averaged 3.8, which is healthy -- users who are arriving are exploring, not bouncing immediately.

Channel composition is the most interesting story this week. Organic Search delivered 65 sessions (46% of total), well above its ~25% baseline expectation. Direct came in at 50 sessions (36%), which is below its typical 60% baseline. Referral held at 20 sessions (14%), above its ~10% norm. Organic Social and Email were minimal, as usual. The Organic Search dominance is a meaningful shift -- it suggests the site is genuinely earning search-driven visitors rather than relying on repeat direct traffic, which is the direction you want. The lower Direct share could simply mean fewer internal team visits this week, or it could reflect a quieter week for any email/social promotion.

GSC impressions and clicks show zero across every day in this window. That is almost certainly a data pipeline issue rather than reality -- the 30-day efficacy rollup shows 234,878 impressions and 1,064 clicks attributed across recent launches, so the search presence is real. This needs to be flagged with whoever manages the GSC integration before next week's briefing.

---

### Trend (first half vs second half)

Sessions nudged upward from 68 in the first half (Aug 5--7) to 72 in the second half (Aug 8--11), a +5.9% improvement. That is technically an acceleration, but the margin is narrow enough that calling it a trend would be overstating it -- flat-to-slightly-up is the honest read. The window is not declining, which is meaningful context given that Aug 8--9 were genuinely soft days (11--15 sessions each). Aug 10 rescued the second half with 29 sessions and 118 pageviews, the highest single-day output of the week, coinciding directly with the batch of page modifications that went live that day.

Pageviews tell the opposite story: 286 in the first half versus 244 in the second. Aug 5's anomalous 162-pageview day (189% above the 28-day median) inflated the first half. Strip that outlier out and the two halves are roughly comparable. The Aug 10 spike in the second half is real and worth watching -- if those modified pages continue to pull traffic as Google re-crawls them, the second half's momentum should carry forward into next week.

---

### Launches + efficacy

Aug 10 was a significant content day. Fourteen pages were modified in a single push, spanning core service pages and blog-style content. Of the four with early GSC data attached, the picture is mixed but not alarming for pages only three days old.

The two pages generating the most concern right now are `/bariatric-surgery-counseling-in-san-antonio-tx/` (1,870 impressions, 0 clicks) and `/testimonials/` (1,436 impressions, 0 clicks). Both are getting surfaced in search -- Google is showing them -- but nobody is clicking through. That is a title tag and meta description problem, not a ranking problem. Something in the snippet is failing to earn the click. The `/bariatric-surgery-psychological-evaluation-in-san-antonio-tx/` page has 1,566 impressions and a 0.4% CTR, which is low but at least non-zero. The `/registered-dietitian-in-san-antonio-tx/` page is the standout at 90 impressions and a 6.7% CTR -- that snippet is working, and the others should be benchmarked against it.

The remaining ten modified pages (bariatric therapy, emotional eating therapy, nutritional counseling, holistic content, "near me" pages, and the two informational posts) have no GSC data surfaced yet, which is expected at three days post-modification. They need another 7--10 days before conclusions are reasonable. The 30-day efficacy rollup shows zero pages in the top 3 or top 10, which is honest -- the site is earning impressions but not yet converting them to rankings that drive volume. That is the central challenge the content strategy needs to address. No new backlinks were earned in this specific window; the backlink profile sits at 4 referring domains, 12 inbound links, and 5 edu links.

---

### What this means + what to do next

1. **Rewrite the title tags and meta descriptions for `/bariatric-surgery-counseling-in-san-antonio-tx/` and `/testimonials/` before anything else.** Both pages have real impression volume and zero clicks -- that is recoverable, but only if the snippets give people a reason to choose BCC over whatever else is on the SERP. Pull the `/registered-dietitian-in-san-antonio-tx/` snippet (6.7% CTR) and use its structure and tone as a working model.

2. **Investigate and resolve the GSC data gap for this window.** Seven straight days of zero impressions in the daily feed while the 30-day rollup shows 234K impressions is a contradiction that makes trend analysis unreliable. If this is a connector/pipeline issue, it needs a fix before the Aug 18 briefing -- otherwise you're flying without instruments on your highest-value signal.

3. **Schedule a CTR audit for the full Aug 10 page batch once they hit 10--14 days live.** That gives Google time to re-index the modifications and stabilize impressions. Around Aug 20--24, run a GSC query filtered to those 14 URLs and look for the pattern: high impressions + low CTR = snippet problem, low impressions = ranking/indexing problem. The two issues need different responses.

4. **Build internal links pointing to the "near me" pages** (`/bariatric-counseling-near-me/` and `/bariatric-therapist-near-me/`). Those pages target high-intent navigational queries. If they are not receiving internal link equity from related service pages and blog posts, they are ranking on their own authority alone -- which, at 4 referring domains sitewide, is limited. Connecting them from the bariatric therapy and bariatric therapist service pages is a straightforward lift.

5. **Begin a backlink outreach plan targeting one or two San Antonio health or university-adjacent organizations.** The profile currently has 5 edu links, which is a genuine asset, but 4 total referring domains is a ceiling on how far organic rankings can climb without external authority signals. One well-placed local health partner link -- a hospital system, a university wellness program, a community health nonprofit -- would meaningfully move the needle at this stage of the site's authority.
