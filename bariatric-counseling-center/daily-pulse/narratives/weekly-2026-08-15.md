# Weekly briefing -- 2026-08-15

### The window in one sentence

The week of August 7--13 delivered 160 sessions at a 22.9-per-day average, with volume accelerating sharply in the second half -- sessions more than doubled from first half to second. The acceleration appears tied to Organic Search leading the channel mix (an inversion of BCC's normal Direct-heavy baseline) and a major August 13 content push across 15+ pages.

---

### Volume + composition

Over the seven-day window, the site logged 160 sessions, 142 users, and 497 pageviews. The daily session average of 22.9 is modest in absolute terms but the distribution underneath that average is what matters -- days ranged from 11 (August 9) to 37 (August 12), so this was not a flat week. GSC impressions and clicks both show zero in the pipeline data for this window, which is almost certainly a data-lag issue rather than a true signal dropout; the 30-day efficacy rollup shows 411,326 impressions and 1,864 clicks attributed to launches, so Search Console is clearly active. Worth confirming the GSC connector is pulling correctly for this date range before drawing any conclusions from those zeros.

Channel composition is the most interesting story here. Organic Search led with 75 sessions (47%), followed by Direct at 61 (38%), Referral at 20 (13%), and a small tail of Organic Social and Email. BCC's established baseline runs approximately 60% Direct / 25% Organic Search / 10% Referral -- this week is essentially inverted on the top two channels, with Organic Search outpacing Direct by nine percentage points. That is a meaningful deviation and a potentially good one. It suggests either a ranking improvement is generating more search-driven traffic, or recent content is pulling impressions it wasn't before. The Direct share dropping below 40% is worth watching -- if it holds low next week, it's a structural shift; if it snaps back, this week was noise.

Referral at 13% is running above baseline and warrants a closer look at source/medium in GA4 to confirm where those 20 sessions originated. The backlinks data (4 referring domains, 12 total inbound links, 5 edu links) suggests there may be some institutional referral activity worth identifying and nurturing.

### Trend (first half vs second half)

This window is clearly accelerating. The first half (August 7--9) produced 53 sessions and 145 pageviews across three days. The second half (August 10--13) produced 107 sessions and 352 pageviews across four days. That's a 101.9% session increase half-over-half -- the site roughly doubled its pace in the back half of the week.

August 12 was the standout day: 37 sessions and 102 pageviews, with new users up 113% versus the 28-day median. One important flag on August 12: session duration dropped 58% below median on that same day (to roughly 52 seconds). High new-user volume paired with low engagement time suggests those visitors may have arrived via a low-intent channel -- possibly an untagged social share or a paid/email push that didn't get proper UTM tagging. The August 13 close at 24 sessions is above median and suggests the lift wasn't a single-day spike that immediately collapsed. The overall directional read here is positive, but the engagement quality on August 12 needs investigation before calling it a clean win.

August 13 was a significant production day. The ledger shows 15 page and post modifications plus two sitemap touches shipped in a single day. Blog posts also touched include the pre-bariatric surgery binge eating screening piece and emotional eating and coping skills.

At two days post-launch, nearly all of these show zero impressions and zero sessions -- which is expected and not a concern at this stage. The one exception worth noting is /food-addiction-treatment-in-san-antonio-tx/, which already has 527 GSC impressions and 7 clicks (1.3% CTR) after just two days. That is early signal worth watching. For context, /post-bariatric-surgery-support/ and /professional-weight-loss-therapy/ each logged 1 GA4 session in the same window. Everything else is at zero, which is appropriate given the timeline.

The therapy landing page cluster (/professional-weight-loss-therapy/, /professional-weight-loss-therapy-2/, /professional-weight-loss-therapy-fb/, /expert-weight-loss-therapy/) is clearly built for paid or ad traffic given the naming conventions and the fb variant. The 30-day efficacy rollup also shows no pages ranking in top 3 or top 10 positions, which tells you the SEO runway is still early despite the impression volume.

The backlink picture is modest but noteworthy: 4 referring domains, 12 total inbound links, and 5 edu links. Edu backlinks at this count are an asset -- .edu domains carry authority that is hard to earn and BCC already has a small collection of them.

### What this means + what to do next

1. **Investigate the August 12 engagement drop before repeating whatever drove traffic that day.** 37 sessions with a 52-second average session duration suggests a high-bounce audience. Pull the source/medium breakdown for August 12 specifically in GA4. If it was an untagged social or email push, get UTMs on it before it goes out again so you can measure it properly next time.

2. **Watch /food-addiction-treatment-in-san-antonio-tx/ closely over the next 10 days.** It's the only August 13 launch showing early GSC traction (527 impressions, 1.3% CTR at day two). If impressions hold or grow by next week's rollup, prioritize internal linking from the binge eating and compulsive overeating cluster pages pointing into it, and audit the meta description for CTR improvement.

3. **Connect the therapy landing pages to actual campaigns or deprioritize them for SEO.** The /professional-weight-loss-therapy/, /professional-weight-loss-therapy-fb/, /professional-weight-loss-therapy-2/, and /expert-weight-loss-therapy/ pages are likely ad landing pages, but ad signals are registering zero. Either confirm the tracking is working if campaigns are live, or hold SEO effort on these pages until campaigns are activated.

4. **Leverage the edu backlink profile.** Five .edu inbound links from four referring domains is a meaningful foundation. Identify which institutions are linking and whether there's a relationship to deepen -- a second link from an established .edu referrer is often easier to earn than a first link from a new one. This is a medium-term play for the next few weeks, not next-day outreach.

5. **Run a GSC data-connection check.** The window data shows zero impressions across all seven days, while the 30-day rollup shows 411,000+ impressions. That discrepancy is almost certainly a connector or date-range issue, not a real traffic collapse. Until that's confirmed, any impression-level analysis for this specific window is unreliable -- which limits your ability to assess which August 13 pages are getting indexed and surfaced in search.
