## What is a bariatric therapist?

A bariatric therapist is a licensed mental health professional who specializes in the behavioral treatment of obesity and weight-related concerns. This type of therapy addresses the psychological and behavioral factors that contribute to overeating, emotional eating, and difficulty maintaining weight loss. At the Bariatric Counseling Center in San Antonio, our staff includes a clinical psychologist, licensed professional counselors, registered dietitians, movement therapists, and a registered nurse who work together in one Intensive Outpatient Program. We provide group and individual therapy, dietary guidance, cooking classes, and movement education for clients struggling with obesity, binge eating, and chronic overeating. The program is available onsite in San Antonio and virtually throughout Texas, and we accept most health insurance plans.
