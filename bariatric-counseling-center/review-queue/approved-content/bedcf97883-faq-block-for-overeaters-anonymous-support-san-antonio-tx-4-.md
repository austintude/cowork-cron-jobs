## Frequently asked questions

**Is Overeaters Anonymous worth it?**

OA meetings offer peer support and community, which many people find valuable. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we complement that approach with professional clinical care: licensed therapists, registered dietitians, cooking classes and movement education in one program. We work with people who attend OA, people who have tried it, and people exploring other paths.

**What is overeating a symptom of?**

Overeating can be tied to stress, anxiety, depression, binge eating disorder, or learned patterns around food. A licensed therapist or psychologist is the right person to explore what is driving it in your specific situation. We help clients understand and modify their relationship with food through behavioral therapy, dietary guidance and coping skills.

**What is the easiest way to stop overeating?**

There is no single easy fix, but understanding the emotional and behavioral patterns behind overeating is a strong foundation. We teach coping skills, mindful eating, nutritional planning and goal setting in group and individual therapy. Recovery takes time, and we provide structured support over roughly three months and 30 sessions in our Intensive Outpatient Program.

**What are the controversies surrounding Overeaters Anonymous?**

Some people appreciate OA's spiritual foundation and 12-step structure. Others prefer a clinical, evidence-based approach with licensed professionals. At BCC, we respect all pathways and do not replace OA. We offer comprehensive behavioral healthcare that can stand alone or work alongside peer support groups, depending on what fits your needs.

If you are looking for professional support for overeating, we are here to help. Reach out to learn more about our program.
