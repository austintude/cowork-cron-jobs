## Frequently asked questions

**Can eating disorders be used as a coping mechanism?**

Yes. Many people use food to cope with stress, anxiety, loneliness, or other difficult emotions. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we help clients understand and modify their relationship with food through behavioral therapy, coping skills training, and mindful eating practices. Our licensed therapists and psychologist work with you to develop healthier ways to manage emotions without turning to food.

**How do I recover from disordered eating?**

Recovery involves understanding the behavioral patterns behind your eating and building new skills. We provide group and individual therapy, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes with a professional chef, and movement education in one Intensive Outpatient Program. Our program runs about three months and roughly 30 sessions, meeting two to three times per week, and is designed to help you develop a sustainable, healthier relationship with food.

**Is treatment covered by insurance?**

We accept most health insurances. Our claims are processed under mental health benefits, and clients typically attend for little to no cost. The Bariatric Counseling Center carries The Joint Commission's Gold Seal of Approval and is in network with most health insurance plans. Cost is one of the most common reasons people delay getting support, and it is worth knowing that this program may already be covered at no cost to you.

If you are struggling with binge eating, emotional eating, or chronic overeating, we are here to help.
