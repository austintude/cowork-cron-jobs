## What is weight management after bariatric surgery?

Weight management after bariatric surgery is the ongoing behavioral and nutritional support needed to maintain weight loss and build sustainable habits following a surgical weight loss procedure. It involves learning to eat intuitively, managing emotional eating patterns, understanding nutritional needs with a smaller stomach, and developing a healthy relationship with movement. At the Bariatric Counseling Center in San Antonio, we provide this support through an Intensive Outpatient Program that includes individual and group therapy with licensed counselors, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes with a professional chef, and movement education. The program is available onsite in San Antonio and virtually throughout Texas, and it is in network with most health insurance plans. Our team coordinates care with your bariatric surgeon to support your behavioral progress after surgery.
