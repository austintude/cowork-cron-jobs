## Frequently asked questions

**What does a bariatric psychologist do?**

A bariatric psychologist helps clients understand and modify their relationship with food through behavioral therapy and counseling. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, our clinical psychologist and licensed professional counselors provide group and individual therapy focused on coping skills, goal setting, mindful eating, and family engagement. We work with clients before or after bariatric surgery, or as standalone behavioral treatment for obesity.

**What is the 30/30 rule for bariatric patients?**

The 30/30 rule and other post-surgery guidelines are medical decisions your bariatric surgeon will discuss with you. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we focus on the behavioral side of obesity treatment. Our registered dietitians provide nutrition education and meal planning, and our team helps clients build sustainable habits around food, eating, and movement that support long-term success.

**What can you never do again after bariatric surgery?**

Dietary restrictions after bariatric surgery are medical questions your surgeon is best equipped to answer. Our role at the Bariatric Counseling Center is to help clients develop coping skills, understand their relationship with food, and build mindful eating habits. We coordinate care with your medical providers and provide behavioral support that complements the medical treatment you receive from your surgical team.

If you are preparing for bariatric surgery or looking for behavioral support after surgery, we are here to help.
