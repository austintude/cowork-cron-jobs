## What does weight loss counseling involve?

Weight loss counseling is behavioral therapy that helps people understand and modify their relationship with food. It involves working with licensed therapists to address binge eating, emotional eating, and chronic overeating through individual and group sessions. Counseling typically includes coping skills, goal setting, and mindful eating practices. At the Bariatric Counseling Center in San Antonio, weight loss counseling is part of an Intensive Outpatient Program that combines behavioral therapy with dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes with a professional chef, and movement education. The program runs about three months, meeting two to three times per week, and is available onsite in San Antonio and virtually throughout Texas, in network with most health insurance plans.
