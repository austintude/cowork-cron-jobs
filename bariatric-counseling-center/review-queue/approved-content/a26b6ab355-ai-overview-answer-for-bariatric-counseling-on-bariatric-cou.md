## What is bariatric counseling?

Bariatric counseling is behavioral therapy designed to help people struggling with obesity, binge eating, and chronic overeating understand and modify their relationship with food. It typically involves working with licensed therapists, dietitians, and other behavioral health professionals to address the psychological and emotional factors that contribute to weight struggles. At the Bariatric Counseling Center in San Antonio, we provide bariatric counseling through an Intensive Outpatient Program that includes group and individual therapy, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes with a professional chef, and movement education. Our program is available onsite in San Antonio and virtually throughout Texas, and we accept most health insurance plans, with claims processed under mental health benefits.
