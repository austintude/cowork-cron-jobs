## Frequently asked questions

**How do I cope with an eating disorder?**

Coping with an eating disorder takes professional support. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we provide group and individual therapy, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes and movement education in one comprehensive program. Our licensed therapists and clinical psychologist help clients develop recovery and coping skills, practice intuitive eating, and understand their relationship with food. We work with people struggling with binge eating, compulsive overeating and chronic overeating, both onsite in San Antonio and virtually across Texas.

**What eating disorder is most common among men and boys?**

Binge eating disorder is the most common eating disorder among men and boys, though it affects people of all genders. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we treat binge eating disorder and compulsive overeating through behavioral therapy, dietary guidance, culinary classes and mindful movement. Our experienced team of licensed professional counselors, a clinical psychologist and registered dietitians provides compassionate, evidence-based care in a supportive environment designed specifically for people who struggle with overeating.

**What are the diagnostic criteria for eating disorders in the DSM-5?**

The DSM-5 lists specific criteria for diagnosing eating disorders, including binge eating disorder, anorexia nervosa and bulimia nervosa. A licensed therapist or psychologist is the right person to provide a formal diagnosis. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, our clinical team includes a licensed psychologist and licensed professional counselors who work with clients struggling with binge eating, emotional eating and chronic overeating. We focus on behavioral treatment and helping clients develop a healthier relationship with food.

If you are struggling with an eating disorder or disordered eating, we are here to help. Reach out to learn more about our program.
