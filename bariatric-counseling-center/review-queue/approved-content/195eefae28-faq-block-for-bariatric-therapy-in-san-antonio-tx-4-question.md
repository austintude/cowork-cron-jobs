## Frequently asked questions

**What does a bariatric psychologist do?**

A bariatric psychologist helps people understand and modify their relationship with food through behavioral therapy. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, our clinical psychologist and licensed professional counselors provide group and individual therapy focused on coping skills, goal setting, mindful eating, and family engagement. We work with clients whether they are preparing for bariatric surgery, currently on weight loss medications, or seeking behavioral treatment for obesity as a standalone approach.

**What therapy is best for weight loss?**

We provide behavioral therapy that addresses the emotional and psychological factors behind overeating, binge eating, and chronic weight struggles. Our approach combines group and individual counseling, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes with a professional chef, and mindful movement education. One program, not four. We coordinate care with your medical providers and offer this comprehensive support both onsite in San Antonio and virtually throughout Texas.

**What are the common causes of anxiety after bariatric surgery?**

Anxiety after bariatric surgery can stem from many sources, and your surgeon or medical provider is the right person to discuss your specific situation. We provide behavioral therapy and counseling to help clients develop coping skills and work through the emotional challenges that come with significant life changes. Our program supports people before surgery, after surgery, or as standalone behavioral treatment for obesity.

**How does bariatric surgery affect mental health?**

Bariatric surgery brings both physical and emotional changes, and the mental health impact varies for each person. We offer behavioral therapy and counseling to support clients through these changes, whether as preparation for surgery, post-surgery support, or alongside weight loss medications. Our licensed professional counselors and clinical psychologist help clients develop recovery and coping skills, practice intuitive eating, and engage family support in a compassionate, structured environment.

If you are looking for behavioral support for weight loss, binge eating, or obesity in San Antonio or anywhere in Texas, we invite you to reach out.
