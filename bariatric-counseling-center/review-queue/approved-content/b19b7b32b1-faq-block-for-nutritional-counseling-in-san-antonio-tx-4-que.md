## Frequently asked questions

**Does insurance cover nutritional counseling?**

We accept most health insurance plans, and our claims are processed under mental health benefits. Because our program includes registered dietitians working alongside licensed therapists and counselors in one Intensive Outpatient Program, clients typically attend for little to no cost. Coverage varies by plan, so we recommend calling us to verify your specific benefits before starting.

**What is the difference between a nutrition counselor and a dietitian?**

A registered dietitian has completed accredited education, clinical training, and national board certification. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, our registered dietitians provide nutritional and meal planning, grocery store tours, and guidance on navigating dining out as part of a multidisciplinary behavioral health team that also includes licensed therapists, a clinical psychologist, and a professional chef.

**Is nutritional counseling worth it?**

We believe nutritional guidance works best when it is part of a comprehensive approach. Our program combines dietary guidance from registered dietitians with behavioral therapy, cooking classes, and movement education in one Intensive Outpatient Program. This helps clients understand and modify their relationship with food in a way that is sustainable, not a quick fix.

**Does insurance cover weight loss therapy?**

Yes. We are in network with most health insurance plans, and our Intensive Outpatient Program is covered under mental health benefits. This program may already be covered at no cost to you. Cost is one of the most common reasons people delay getting support, so it is worth calling to verify your benefits and get started.

If you have questions about coverage or whether our program is right for you, reach out and we will walk you through next steps.
