## What support is available for overeaters in San Antonio?

Overeaters are people who struggle with chronic overeating, binge eating, emotional eating, or compulsive eating patterns. This often involves eating beyond physical hunger, difficulty stopping once started, and using food to cope with stress or emotions. The Bariatric Counseling Center provides behavioral treatment for overeating through an Intensive Outpatient Program that includes group and individual therapy, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes with a professional chef, and movement education. The program runs onsite in San Antonio and virtually across Texas, meeting two to three times per week for about three months. We are in network with most health insurance plans, and claims are processed under mental health benefits.
