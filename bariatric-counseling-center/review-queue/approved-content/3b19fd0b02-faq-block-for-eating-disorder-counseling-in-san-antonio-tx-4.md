## Frequently asked questions

**What are the top 3 most common eating disorders?**

The most common eating disorders include binge eating disorder, bulimia nervosa, and anorexia nervosa. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we work with clients struggling with binge eating, compulsive overeating, and chronic over-eating. Our intensive outpatient program provides behavioral therapy, dietary guidance, cooking classes, and movement education in one comprehensive format led by licensed counselors, a clinical psychologist, and registered dietitians.

**What kind of therapy is best for eating disorders?**

Effective eating disorder treatment typically combines behavioral therapy with nutritional support and lifestyle education. We provide group and individual therapy, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes with a professional chef, and mindful movement education. Our approach helps clients understand and modify their relationship with food. A licensed therapist or psychologist can recommend the specific treatment approach that fits your needs.

**Where can I find an eating disorder therapist in San Antonio?**

The Bariatric Counseling Center offers eating disorder counseling both onsite in San Antonio and virtually throughout Texas. We provide an intensive outpatient program that runs about three months, meeting two to three times per week, and we accept most health insurance plans.
