## What is nutrition counseling?

Nutrition counseling is one-on-one or group education with a registered dietitian to help people improve their eating habits and relationship with food. It involves meal planning, grocery shopping guidance, learning to navigate eating out, and developing sustainable nutrition strategies tailored to individual needs. At the Bariatric Counseling Center in San Antonio, registered dietitians provide nutrition counseling as part of a comprehensive behavioral health program for people struggling with obesity, binge eating, and chronic overeating. We offer nutritional guidance both onsite at our San Antonio facility and virtually throughout Texas. Sessions include meal planning support, grocery store tours, and practical strategies for dining out. The program is in network with most health insurance plans, and claims are processed under mental health benefits.
