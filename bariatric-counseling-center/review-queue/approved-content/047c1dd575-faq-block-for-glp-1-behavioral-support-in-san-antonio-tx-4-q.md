## Frequently asked questions

**Can mental health providers prescribe GLP-1 medications?**

No. Prescribing weight loss medications is a medical decision made by your physician or nurse practitioner. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we provide behavioral therapy, dietary guidance, cooking classes and mindful movement to support people taking weight loss medications. We coordinate care with your medical provider but do not prescribe or manage medication.

**Where can I get GLP-1 medication in San Antonio?**

Your primary care physician, endocrinologist or weight management specialist can evaluate whether weight loss medication is right for you. We focus on the behavioral side: helping you build sustainable eating habits, manage emotional eating and understand your relationship with food while you are on medication. We work alongside your medical provider.

**Are there support groups for people on GLP-1 medications?**

Yes. We offer group and individual therapy, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes with a professional chef, and movement education in one Intensive Outpatient Program. Our program serves people struggling with weight loss, binge eating and emotional eating, including those taking weight loss medications. Sessions meet two to three times per week, onsite in San Antonio or virtually across Texas.

**Does insurance cover behavioral support for weight loss medications?**

We accept most health insurance plans, and our claims are processed under mental health benefits. Many clients attend for little to no cost. Coverage depends on your individual plan. Our team can verify your benefits and explain what is covered before you begin the program.

If you have questions about how our program can support you, we are here to help.
