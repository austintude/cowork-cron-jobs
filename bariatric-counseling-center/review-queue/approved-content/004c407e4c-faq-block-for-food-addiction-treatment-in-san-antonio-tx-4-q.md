## Frequently asked questions

**What are the 4 C's of addiction?**

The 4 C's are craving, loss of control, compulsion, and continued use despite consequences. These patterns show up in food addiction just as they do in other addictive behaviors. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we help clients understand and modify their relationship with food through group and individual therapy, dietary guidance, cooking classes, and mindful movement education.

**How can I mentally stop food cravings?**

Managing food cravings involves learning coping skills, identifying triggers, and practicing mindful eating. Our program teaches recovery and coping skills in a supportive environment with licensed therapists, registered dietitians, and a professional chef. We help clients develop sustainable strategies to respond to cravings without relying on willpower alone. Real change comes from understanding why cravings happen and having tools to address them.

**What is the most common food addiction?**

People often struggle with highly processed foods that combine sugar, fat, and salt. But food addiction is not really about one specific food. It is about the relationship someone has with eating. We focus on helping people who struggle with binge eating, compulsive overeating, and chronic over-eating, regardless of which foods are involved.

**What support groups exist for people with eating problems?**

We provide group therapy as part of our Intensive Outpatient Program, where clients meet two to three times per week for about three months. Our groups are led by licensed professional counselors and a clinical psychologist, not peer-led. This structure allows for clinical guidance alongside the peer support that makes group work so valuable.

If you are struggling with food addiction or compulsive overeating, we are here to help. Reach out to learn more about our program.
