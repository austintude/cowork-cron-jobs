## Frequently asked questions

**What therapy is best for weight loss?**

Behavioral therapy that helps you understand and modify your relationship with food works well for many people. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we combine group and individual therapy, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes with a professional chef, and movement education in one comprehensive program. Our approach addresses the emotional, nutritional, and behavioral aspects of weight management together, not separately.

**What are some effective weight loss strategies?**

Sustainable weight loss often comes from addressing multiple areas at once. We focus on developing coping skills, learning intuitive and mindful eating, getting practical nutrition education, and finding enjoyable ways to move. Our clients work with licensed therapists, registered dietitians, and movement educators in an Intensive Outpatient Program that runs about three months. The goal is building skills that last beyond the program itself.

**Does exercise help with weight loss?**

Movement is one part of a larger picture. We teach mindful movement, which means finding physical activity that feels good and fits your life, rather than focusing only on calorie burn. Our movement therapists help clients explore enjoyable exercise options and build a sustainable plan. Behavioral therapy, dietary guidance, and cooking skills work together with movement to support long-term change.

If you are struggling with weight loss, binge eating, or emotional eating, we are here to help. Reach out to learn more about our program.
