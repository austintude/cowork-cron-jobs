## Frequently asked questions

**What should I do if I can't stop overeating?**

If you find yourself struggling with overeating, reaching out for professional support is a good first step. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we provide group and individual therapy, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes and movement education to help people understand and modify their relationship with food. Our program addresses the behavioral patterns behind overeating in a compassionate, structured environment.

**How can I train my brain to stop overeating?**

Changing patterns around food takes more than willpower. It involves learning new coping skills, understanding emotional triggers, and building sustainable habits. Our therapists and dietitians work with clients on mindful eating, goal setting, and practical strategies that make lasting change possible. We teach these skills in both individual sessions and group settings, so you have ongoing support as you build new patterns.

**What is overeating a symptom of?**

Overeating can be connected to many things, from stress and emotional coping to learned habits and food environments. A licensed therapist or your medical provider can help identify what may be contributing in your specific situation. Our program addresses the behavioral and emotional aspects of overeating through counseling, nutrition education, and skill-building in a supportive setting designed specifically for people struggling with weight loss and food-related concerns.

**What is the root cause of binge eating?**

Binge eating often stems from a combination of emotional, behavioral, and environmental factors. There is rarely just one cause. Our clinical psychologist and licensed professional counselors help clients explore what drives their eating patterns and develop healthier coping skills. We work on these issues through individual therapy, group support, and practical education around food, nutrition, and mindful eating in our Intensive Outpatient Program.

If you are struggling with compulsive overeating or food addiction, we are here to help. Reach out to learn more about our program.
