## Frequently asked questions

**What is a psychological evaluation for bariatric surgery?**

A psychological evaluation assesses whether you are ready for surgery and helps identify any behavioral or emotional factors that may affect your success. At BCC, our licensed therapist or clinical psychologist conducts a thorough assessment and provides a detailed letter to your surgeon. We also offer ongoing behavioral support through our Intensive Outpatient Program.

**Why do I need a psych eval for bariatric surgery?**

Most surgeons require a psychological evaluation to ensure you are prepared for the lifestyle changes surgery requires. The evaluation looks at your relationship with food, emotional eating patterns, coping skills, and readiness for long-term behavioral change. Your surgeon uses this information to decide if surgery is appropriate and to plan your care.

**What does a psych eval for bariatric surgery look like?**

The evaluation typically includes a clinical interview with a licensed therapist or psychologist, along with written assessments that measure eating behaviors, mood, and quality of life. We review your medical history, weight history, and goals. Afterward, we send a letter to your surgeon with our findings and recommendations for any additional behavioral support you may benefit from.

**Is there an online psychological evaluation available for bariatric surgery?**

Yes. We offer virtual psychological evaluations via telehealth for clients throughout Texas. The evaluation is conducted by video appointment with one of our licensed professionals. Whether you meet with us onsite in San Antonio or virtually, the assessment is the same thorough process, and we provide the documentation your surgeon requires.

**How do I prepare for a bariatric surgery psychological evaluation?**

Be honest and open during the interview. There is no pass or fail. We are looking to understand your relationship with food, your support system, and your readiness for change. Bring a list of any medications you take and be prepared to discuss your weight history, eating patterns, and what you hope surgery will accomplish for you.

If you would like to schedule a bariatric surgery psychological evaluation or learn more about our program, we invite you to reach out.
