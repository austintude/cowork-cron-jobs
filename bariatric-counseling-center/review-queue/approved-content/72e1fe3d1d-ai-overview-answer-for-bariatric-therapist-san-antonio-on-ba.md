## What is bariatric therapy?

Bariatric therapy is behavioral treatment for obesity, binge eating and chronic overeating. It typically involves working with licensed therapists and registered dietitians to understand and modify your relationship with food, develop coping skills, and build sustainable eating and movement habits. At the Bariatric Counseling Center in San Antonio, we provide this care through an Intensive Outpatient Program that combines group and individual therapy, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes with a professional chef, and movement education. Our program runs about three months, meeting two to three times per week, and is available both onsite at our San Antonio facility and virtually throughout Texas. We are in network with most health insurance plans, with claims processed under mental health benefits.
