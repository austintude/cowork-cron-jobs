## Frequently asked questions

**Who should I see for binge eating?**

At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we provide group and individual therapy with licensed professional counselors and a clinical psychologist who specialize in binge eating and other eating behaviors. Our program also includes registered dietitians, cooking classes with a professional chef, and mindful movement education. One program, not four.

**Where can I find an eating disorder therapist in San Antonio?**

We offer specialized behavioral therapy for binge eating disorder, compulsive overeating, and food addiction at our San Antonio facility and virtually throughout Texas. Our experienced and compassionate team includes licensed professional counselors, a clinical psychologist, and registered dietitians. We accept most health insurance plans, and clients typically attend for little to no cost.

**What are the reasons why people binge eat?**

Binge eating has many different causes, and understanding your personal relationship with food is part of the work we do together. Our behavioral therapy helps clients develop coping skills, practice mindful eating, and address the emotions and patterns connected to binge eating. A licensed therapist can help you explore the specific factors affecting you.

**Why can't I stop overeating?**

Stopping overeating on your own can feel impossible because food serves many roles beyond nutrition. Our program helps you understand and modify your relationship with food through behavioral counseling, dietary guidance, cooking instruction, and movement education. We work with you to build sustainable coping skills and a healthier approach to eating in a supportive environment.

If you are struggling with binge eating, we are here to help. Reach out to learn more about our program.
