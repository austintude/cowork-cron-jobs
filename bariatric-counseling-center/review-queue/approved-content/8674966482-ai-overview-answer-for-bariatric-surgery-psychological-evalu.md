## What is a bariatric surgery psychological evaluation?

A bariatric surgery psychological evaluation is an assessment that determines whether a person is psychologically prepared for weight loss surgery and able to make the behavioral changes required for long-term success. The evaluation typically includes a clinical interview, review of eating behaviors, assessment of mental health history, and discussion of post-surgery expectations and support systems. At the Bariatric Counseling Center in San Antonio, our clinical psychologist and licensed professional counselors conduct these evaluations onsite and virtually throughout Texas. We coordinate care with referring surgeons and provide detailed reports to support the surgical team's decision-making process. Most health insurance plans cover these evaluations under mental health benefits.
