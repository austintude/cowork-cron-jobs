## Frequently asked questions

**What kind of therapy helps with weight loss?**

Behavioral therapy helps people understand and modify their relationship with food. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we provide group and individual therapy focused on recovery and coping skills, intuitive eating, goal setting, and mindful eating. Our licensed therapists work with clients to address emotional eating, binge eating, and chronic over-eating in a supportive environment.

**What is a weight management doctor for?**

A weight management doctor provides medical treatment for obesity, which may include weight loss medications or bariatric surgery. At BCC, we offer the behavioral healthcare side. Our program can be standalone behavioral treatment for obesity, or in addition to medical treatment that clients receive from their doctors. We coordinate care with referring medical providers throughout the program.

**What's good for weight management?**

Sustainable weight management requires addressing the behavioral and emotional aspects of eating. We provide behavioral therapy, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes with a professional chef, and mindful movement education. One program, not four. Our Intensive Outpatient Program runs about three months, meeting two to three times per week, and is covered by most health insurance plans.

If you are struggling with weight loss or emotional eating, we are here to help. Reach out to learn more about our program.
