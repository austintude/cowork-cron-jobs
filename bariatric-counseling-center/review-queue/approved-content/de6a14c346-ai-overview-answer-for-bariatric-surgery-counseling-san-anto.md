## Bariatric Surgery Counseling in San Antonio

Bariatric surgery counseling is behavioral health support that helps people prepare for or maintain weight loss after bariatric surgery. It involves working with licensed therapists and registered dietitians to understand and modify your relationship with food, develop coping skills, practice mindful eating, and plan sustainable nutrition and movement habits. The Bariatric Counseling Center provides this support through an Intensive Outpatient Program that includes group and individual therapy, dietary guidance, cooking classes, and movement education. We serve clients onsite in San Antonio and virtually across Texas. The program runs about three months, meeting two to three times per week, and is in network with most health insurance plans. Our claims are processed under mental health benefits, so clients typically attend for little to no cost.
