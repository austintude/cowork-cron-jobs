## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best therapy for weight loss?**

The best therapy addresses the behaviors and emotions behind overeating. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we combine group and individual therapy, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes and movement education in one comprehensive program. We help clients understand and modify their relationship with food. A licensed therapist can help you determine which approach fits your needs.

**How do I get over the mental block of weight loss?**

Mental blocks around weight often stem from emotional eating, past struggles, or anxiety about change. Our therapists work with clients to build coping skills, set realistic goals and practice mindful eating. We address the emotional side of food in both group and individual sessions. Progress takes time, but you do not have to work through it alone.

**How do I talk to someone about losing weight?**

If you are the one struggling, reaching out to a licensed therapist or counselor is a strong first step. Our team listens without judgment and helps you explore what is driving your relationship with food. If you are supporting someone else, focus on care and concern, not criticism. Let them lead the conversation and offer to help them find resources.

If you are ready to explore how counseling can support your goals, reach out to us. We are here to help.
