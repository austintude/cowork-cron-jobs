## Frequently asked questions

**What is the root cause of binge eating?**

Binge eating often stems from a combination of emotional, psychological, and environmental factors. Stress, trauma, dieting patterns, and learned coping behaviors can all play a role. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, our licensed therapists and registered dietitians work with clients to understand their unique triggers and develop healthier ways to relate to food and emotions.

**What is the difference between extreme hunger and binge eating?**

Extreme hunger is a physical need for food, often after prolonged restriction or intense activity. Binge eating typically involves eating large amounts quickly, often past fullness, and may feel out of control. Our team helps clients learn to distinguish physical hunger from emotional eating and develop intuitive eating skills that honor their body's signals.

**Why can't I stop overeating?**

Overeating is rarely about willpower. It often reflects deeper emotional patterns, stress responses, or a disconnect from hunger and fullness cues. We provide group and individual therapy, dietary guidance, and mindful eating education to help clients understand and modify their relationship with food. One program, not four.

If you are struggling with binge eating or chronic overeating, we are here to help. Reach out to learn more about our program.
