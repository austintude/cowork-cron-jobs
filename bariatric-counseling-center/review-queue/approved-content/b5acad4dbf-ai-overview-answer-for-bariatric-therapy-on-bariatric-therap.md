## What is bariatric therapy?

Bariatric therapy is behavioral treatment for obesity that addresses the psychological, nutritional and lifestyle factors affecting weight. It involves individual and group counseling, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, education about food preparation and mindful eating, and movement planning. At the Bariatric Counseling Center in San Antonio, we provide bariatric therapy through an Intensive Outpatient Program that runs about three months and roughly 30 sessions, meeting two to three times per week. Our staff is comprised of a clinical psychologist, licensed professional counselors, registered dietitians, movement therapists, and a registered nurse, as well as a professional chef. The program is available onsite at our San Antonio facility and virtually throughout Texas, in network with most health insurance plans.
