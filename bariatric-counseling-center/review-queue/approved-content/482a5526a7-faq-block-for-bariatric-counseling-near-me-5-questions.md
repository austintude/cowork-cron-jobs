## Frequently asked questions

**What is behavioral therapy for obesity?**

Behavioral therapy for obesity helps you understand and modify your relationship with food through counseling, coping skills, goal setting, and mindful eating. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we provide group and individual therapy, dietary guidance, cooking classes, and movement education in one Intensive Outpatient Program, led by an experienced and compassionate team of behavioral healthcare professionals.

**What does a bariatric psychologist do?**

A bariatric psychologist works with clients on the emotional and behavioral aspects of weight struggles, including binge eating, emotional eating, and chronic overeating. Our clinical psychologist and licensed professional counselors provide therapy focused on recovery and coping skills, intuitive eating, and family engagement, helping clients build sustainable habits and a healthier relationship with food.

**What does a bariatric dietitian do?**

A bariatric dietitian provides nutritional education, meal planning, and practical support tailored to your needs. Our registered dietitians lead grocery store tours, teach you how to navigate dining out, and work alongside our professional chef in cooking classes. We focus on sustainable dietary changes that fit your life, not rigid rules or restrictive meal plans.

**What therapy is best for weight loss?**

The most effective therapy addresses the behavioral and emotional patterns behind overeating. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we combine behavioral therapy, dietary guidance, cooking classes, and movement education in one program. This comprehensive approach, delivered by healthcare professionals in four disciplines, helps clients build lasting skills. One program, not four.

**What is the mental health evaluation for bariatric surgery?**

Most bariatric surgery programs require a psychological evaluation to assess readiness and identify any behavioral or emotional factors that may affect outcomes. If you are preparing for surgery or already had it, our Intensive Outpatient Program can provide the behavioral support you need. Your surgeon decides the medical requirements, and we coordinate care with your medical providers throughout treatment.

If you have questions about our program or would like to learn more, we invite you to reach out.
