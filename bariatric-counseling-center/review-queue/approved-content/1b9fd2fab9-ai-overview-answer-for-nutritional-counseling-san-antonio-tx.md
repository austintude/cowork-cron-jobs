## What is nutritional counseling for weight management?

Nutritional counseling for weight management is one-on-one or group education with a registered dietitian who helps you plan meals, understand portion sizes, and build sustainable eating habits. It typically includes meal planning, grocery shopping guidance, and support for navigating restaurants and social eating situations. At the Bariatric Counseling Center in San Antonio, registered dietitians provide nutritional counseling as part of an Intensive Outpatient Program that also includes behavioral therapy, cooking classes with a professional chef, and movement education.
