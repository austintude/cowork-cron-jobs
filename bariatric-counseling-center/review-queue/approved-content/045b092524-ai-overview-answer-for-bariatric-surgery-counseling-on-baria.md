## What is bariatric surgery counseling?

Bariatric surgery counseling is behavioral health support that helps people prepare for weight loss surgery or address eating and emotional challenges after the procedure. It typically involves working with licensed therapists, psychologists, and registered dietitians to understand eating patterns, build coping skills, and develop sustainable habits around food and movement. At the Bariatric Counseling Center in San Antonio, we provide this counseling as part of our Intensive Outpatient Program, available onsite at our San Antonio facility and virtually throughout Texas. Our program includes group and individual therapy, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes with a professional chef, and movement education. We coordinate care with medical providers and are in network with most health insurance plans, with claims processed under mental health benefits.
