## Frequently asked questions

**How can I train my brain to stop overeating?**

We help clients understand and modify their relationship with food through behavioral therapy, mindful eating education, and dietary guidance from registered dietitians. Our Intensive Outpatient Program teaches recovery and coping skills you can use long after treatment ends. A licensed therapist can work with you on the specific thoughts and behaviors driving overeating.

**How do I stop my cravings and overeating?**

Stopping cravings and overeating takes more than willpower. We provide group and individual therapy, dietary guidance, cooking classes, and mindful eating education in one program. Our team helps clients build coping skills, set realistic goals, and create a sustainable plan. You work with a clinical psychologist, licensed professional counselors, and registered dietitians who understand the behavioral side of food.

**Why do people binge?**

Binge eating has many causes, including emotional triggers, stress, past dieting, and the way our brains respond to certain foods. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we focus on understanding those patterns and learning new ways to cope. Our behavioral therapy and counseling help clients address the underlying reasons they turn to food, not just the eating itself.

**Where does binge eating start?**

Binge eating often starts with a combination of emotional, environmental, and biological factors. It can develop after years of dieting, during stressful life events, or as a way to manage difficult feelings. We help clients trace those patterns and build healthier responses. A licensed therapist or psychologist is the right person to explore your individual history and triggers.

If you are struggling with binge eating or chronic overeating, we are here to help. Reach out to learn more about our program.
