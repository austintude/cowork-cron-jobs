## Frequently asked questions

**What are the top 3 most common eating disorders?**

The most commonly diagnosed eating disorders are anorexia nervosa, bulimia nervosa, and binge eating disorder. At the Bariatric Counseling Center, we work with clients who struggle with binge eating, compulsive overeating, and emotional eating. Our program provides behavioral therapy, dietary guidance, cooking classes, and movement education to help clients understand and modify their relationship with food.

**Can eating disorders be used as a coping mechanism?**

Many people use food as a way to cope with stress, anxiety, or difficult emotions. Our program focuses on helping clients develop healthier coping skills through individual and group therapy. We work with licensed professional counselors and a clinical psychologist to address the behavioral patterns behind emotional eating and chronic overeating, providing tools that support long-term change.

**What are the symptoms of ARFID?**

Avoidant Restrictive Food Intake Disorder involves limited eating that is not related to body image concerns. While ARFID requires diagnosis by a medical provider, our program addresses many eating-related behavioral challenges. If you are struggling with disordered eating patterns of any kind, we encourage you to speak with your medical provider about whether our behavioral program might be appropriate for you.

If you are struggling with eating concerns, we are here to help. Reach out to learn more about our program.
