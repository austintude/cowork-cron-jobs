# Texas food addiction and binge eating: what people search for (2026-Q3)

Every quarter we count how many people in Texas turn to Google for help with overeating, binge eating, food addiction, eating disorders and the counseling side of bariatric surgery and weight loss. The numbers below come from Google Ads search volume for Texas, Sep 2025 to Aug 2026. Media, partners and directories are welcome to cite them with a link.

- 37,440 Texas searches a month across 80 terms.

## Methodology

Search volume is the Google Ads monthly average for each term, geo-targeted to Texas (and to the United States for the share figure), retrieved through the DataForSEO Keyword Data API. Month range: Sep 2025 to Aug 2026. Terms with under 10 Texas searches a month are left out. Close variants that Google Ads reports with identical volume are counted once. Each term belongs to one topic, assigned by keyword pattern. Dictionary lookups, films, brand names and surgeon searches are excluded. The 12-month change compares the latest month with the first month of the series. Page built 2026-09-18 for 2026-Q3.

Source: Bariatric Counseling Center, San Antonio. Data: Google Ads search volume, Texas, Sep 2025 to Aug 2026.
