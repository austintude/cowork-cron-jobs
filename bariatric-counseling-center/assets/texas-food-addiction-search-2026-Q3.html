Methodology

Search volume is the Google Ads monthly average for each term, geo-targeted to Texas (and to the United States for the share figure), retrieved through the DataForSEO Keyword Data API. Month range: Sep 2025 to Aug 2026. Terms with under 10 Texas searches a month are left out. Close variants that Google Ads reports with identical volume (plurals, word order) are counted once. Each term belongs to one topic, assigned by keyword pattern in this order: Food addiction, Binge eating, Overeating and emotional eating, Eating disorders (anorexia, bulimia, ARFID, night eating), Bariatric counseling and psychological evaluation, Weight loss counseling and therapy. Dictionary lookups, films, brand names and surgeon searches are excluded. The 12-month change compares the latest month with the first month of the series. Questions come from Google's People also ask feature on 20 tracked terms checked in San Antonio and Texas. Page built 2026-09-18 for 2026-Q3.

Source: Bariatric Counseling Center, San Antonio. Data: Google Ads search volume, Texas, Sep 2025 to Aug 2026.
