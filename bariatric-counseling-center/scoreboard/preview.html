Bariatric surgery counseling is behavioral health support that helps people prepare for weight loss surgery or address eating and emotional challenges after the procedure. It typically involves working with licensed therapists, psychologists, and registered dietitians to understand eating patterns, build coping skills, and develop sustainable habits around food and movement. ## What is bariatric counseling?

Bariatric counseling is behavioral therapy designed to help people struggling with obesity, binge eating, and chronic overeating understand and modify their relationship with food. It typically involves working with licensed therapists, dietitians, and other behavioral health professionals to address the psychological and emotional factors that contribute to weight struggles. At the Bariatric Counseling Center in San Antonio, we provide bariatric counseling through an Intensive Outpatient Program that includes group and individual therapy, dietary guidance from registered dietitians, cooking classes with a professional chef, and movement education. ## What is nutrition counseling?

Nutrition counseling is one-on-one or group education with a registered dietitian to help people improve their eating habits and relationship with food.
