## Proposed additions to the existing post (for approval)

### 1. Direct answer, as a new H2 at the top of the first section

**What is stress eating?**

Stress eating is eating in response to stress or difficult emotions rather than physical hunger. It often shows up in the evening, when the day finally slows down, and usually involves quick, familiar comfort foods. It is a learned coping pattern, not a character flaw, and it can be changed with behavioral support and new coping skills.

### 2. FAQ block (appended to the post, Rank Math FAQ schema)

**Why do I eat when I am stressed?**
Stress raises the urge to seek quick comfort, and food is fast, familiar and always available. Over time the brain learns to link stress with eating. Noticing that link is the first step to changing it.

**How do I stop stress eating at night?**
Notice the moment the day catches up with you, name what you actually need (rest, quiet, company), and plan a response that does not involve the kitchen. A regular evening routine and enough food earlier in the day also help.

**When is stress eating a sign I need help?**
If it happens most days, leaves you feeling out of control, or comes with binge eating or shame, it is worth talking to a professional.
